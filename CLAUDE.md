# Bloody Brilliant: notes for Claude

## Design rule: no text in the story

The only text in the story is the title at the start: **BLOODY BRILLIANT.** and its tagline.
Nothing else may write words on screen in any chapter, now or in the future:

- no chapter names or headings
- no captions, hints or instructions (e.g. "SCROLL, CLICK, PLAY")
- no toasts or pop-up words (chaos, mode switch, multiverse beats)
- no speech bubbles (the troll is silent)
- no words drawn on the canvas (`fillText`), not even as part of a scene

Tell the story with pictures, motion and sound instead. UI chrome outside the story is fine:
the header (logo, kids/grown-ups switch, SOUND, ABOUT, credit), the burger menu, the sound pop-up,
the parental gate and the About page.

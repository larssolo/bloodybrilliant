What is 7 × 8?

An endless, scroll-driven interactive story

Bloody Brilliant: The Unreal of North is a free interactive story that runs in the browser. You scroll, and the world moves; you click or tap, and it answers. It begins as a pastel Nordic fairy tale and, when you scroll past the end, the world is reborn with a new seed and the story starts over. It never ends. Made by Lars Sohl of KodeKid. About the story and the maker.

The chapters

  1. The Unreal North: day turns to sunset and northern lights, mountains with faces, a troll behind the hill, a Viking balloon, the rainbow bridge Bifrost, a sea serpent and a moose with blooming antlers.
  2. The Singing Sky: warp stars, aurora that bends towards the cursor, a whale made of constellations that sings, and jellyfish.
  3. Lantern Woods: a parallax forest of glowing mushrooms and swinging lanterns, and moths that follow you.
  4. Sunfall: the sun crashes, the sky burns amber and it rains ink (grown-up mode).
  5. Bloody Brilliant: a raven of ink that dissolves into smoke (grown-up mode).
  6. Ragnarök: Yggdrasil, burning runes, Odin, Huginn and Muninn, and Fenrir breaking free (grown-up mode).
  7. The Ink: the eye of Odin opens and follows you (grown-up mode).
  8. Prism: a sideways world of Dalí-like rainbow horses on stilts, floating islands and waterfalls that you can paint with colour.
  9. The Multiverse of Nonsense: figures escape a vector drawing and live by their own physics, until everything is pulled into a vortex and the world is reborn.

Kids mode is the default and skips the dark chapters through a rainbow portal. Grown-ups can unlock them behind a small parental gate. Each chapter has its own music, and sound effects are synthesised live in the browser.
